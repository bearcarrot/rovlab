# RoV LAB — Legal & Attribution Framework

> Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

> This document is a project documentation framework, not legal advice.

## Independent Project

RoV LAB is an independent community project.

It should not be presented as an official website, application, service, or product of Garena or the relevant RoV rights holder.

## Intellectual Property

Game names, logos, characters, artwork, icons, trademarks, and other game-related intellectual property remain the property of their respective owners.

Using an external asset in RoV LAB does not by itself mean that RoV LAB owns that asset.

## Disclaimer

RoV LAB provides game information, analysis tools, community features, and AI-assisted tools for informational and gameplay-planning purposes.

The project does not guarantee:

- accuracy
- completeness
- current patch compatibility
- uninterrupted availability
- correctness of AI analysis
- correctness of tier/counter/build recommendations
- any particular gameplay outcome

Features such as Tier List, Counter Pick, Matchup, Item Build, Draft Assistant, and AI Coach are analytical aids.

## Data Attribution

Where practical, RoV LAB should identify:

- source
- source category
- patch/version
- verification date

The project should not claim ownership of third-party data or assets where ownership has not been established.

If licensing or permitted use of an asset is unclear, verify the applicable source terms before using it, especially for commercial distribution.

## Privacy Policy

Before publishing a public Privacy Policy, document the application's actual data flows.

Potential areas include:

- account registration
- authentication providers
- profile information
- uploaded avatars
- comments
- community activity
- favorites
- logs
- analytics
- third-party services
- data retention
- deletion requests
- applicable user rights
- contact/reporting channels

Do not publish a fictional privacy policy that describes data the application does not actually collect or protect.

## Terms of Use

A public Terms of Use should address, as applicable:

- permitted use
- account responsibilities
- prohibited abuse
- community content
- malicious links
- impersonation
- intellectual property
- service availability
- limitations
- policy changes
- contact/reporting

The final terms should reflect the actual service and applicable law.

## Community Guidelines

Community features should prohibit or moderate content such as:

- harassment
- hate or discriminatory content
- spam
- impersonation
- malicious links
- illegal content
- deliberately misleading claims presented as official information

## Footer

The website footer should remain concise.

Recommended links:

- About RoV LAB
- Data Sources
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Community Guidelines
- Contact / Report

Detailed explanations belong on dedicated pages rather than making the footer excessively large.
